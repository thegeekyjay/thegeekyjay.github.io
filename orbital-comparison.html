Apollo 13  vs  Artemis 2

Lunar Trajectory Comparison — Not to Scale
Apollo 13 — 1970
Free-return trajectory. Looped around the far side of the Moon at ~254 km altitude after an O₂ tank explosion forced abort.
Max distance from Earth: ~400,171 km
Mission duration: ~5.9 days
Artemis 2 — 2025 (planned)
Hybrid free-return trajectory. Swings ~9,200 km beyond the Moon — the farthest crewed flight ever attempted.
Max distance from Earth: ~457,000 km
Mission duration: ~10 days
