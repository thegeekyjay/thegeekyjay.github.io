Verdict

OnePlus 15 — Best Value

Most storage, fastest charging, biggest battery. The 16GB/512GB config at €865 makes the competition look overpriced on specs. Trade-off: shorter software support and heavier at 211–215g.

Google Pixel 10 — Best Software

Cleanest Android experience with 7 years of guaranteed updates. Hard to justify at €919 for only 128GB storage. Best pick if longevity and software quality matter more than raw specs.

Samsung Galaxy S26 — Most Refined

Lightest flagship available at just 167g, 7-year updates, and Samsung's polished One UI. Sits €19 over the €1,000 ceiling — worth stretching for if compactness and build quality are priorities.
